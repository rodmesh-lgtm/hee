# Business Model

## Pilot
- 3 independent merchants
- 4 branches
- Pickup only
- Saudi Arabia

## Merchant promise
HEE prepares the storefront, menu, QR and order workflow.

## Customer promise
Order and pay before arrival, then collect when ready.

## Pilot success metrics
- First paid order
- Orders per branch
- Checkout completion
- Average preparation time
- Order failure rate
- Repeat customer rate
